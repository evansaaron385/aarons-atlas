Meet Aaron

I’m an environmental scientist and GIS professional who enjoys turning geospatial data into practical environmental solutions. My work has included remote sensing, hydrology mapping, and natural resource management using ArcGIS Pro, LiDAR, and aerial imagery to support decision-making across large forest landscapes.

Alongside my civilian experience, six years as a CBRN NCO in the Army National Guard strengthened my leadership, problem-solving, and emergency response skills. Whether I’m building maps, analyzing environmental data, or exploring new places with my camera, I’m driven by curiosity and a passion for understanding the world around us.
